# UI review: built site (visual and premium feel), 2026-09-19

Scope: the built pages served over `python -m http.server` at 1440 px and 375 px. Pages: index, timeline, glossary,
compare, about, academy, map, 404 and the 5 temple pages. `node scripts/build.mjs --check` reports that everything is up to date (13 pages).
Method: I took full-height headless Chrome screenshots and read each one. I also ran an in-page probe for overflow, font sizes below 14 px, targets smaller than 44 px, "undefined"/"null" text and broken images, and measured the compare silhouettes.
Screenshot wrappers were kept in the session scratch dir and served same-origin by a scratch proxy, so nothing was written to `dev/preview/`.

## Verdict (5 lines)

1. Nothing is broken at the rendering level. No horizontal scroll at 375 px, no broken images, no "undefined"/"null" text, one h1 per page.
2. The "not premium" feeling comes from density. Every claim shows its full "Note:" paragraph, and some notes are internal fact-checker remarks ("Never use for size comparisons.").
3. The temple pages have no accordions, so Konark runs to 7,400 px on desktop and 12,600 px on mobile. Content sits in a left 68ch column with the right 40% empty.
4. Two visual bugs: compare silhouettes are not drawn to scale, and the home "Evolution Story" puts each era's heading under its text and leaves an orphaned 6th column.
5. Three of the seven nav items lead to empty or "being built" pages, and every "Parts of this temple" pill is a dead link.

**Counts:** Blocker 2 · Major 17 · Polish 11

## Top 10 fixes that would most improve the premium feel (in order)

1. **Collapse every "Note:" behind a closed disclosure** (M1), and send the internal-language notes back through the fact-check pipeline (B2). This one change removes most of the text walls.
2. **Turn the temple sections into real `<details>` accordions and move to a two-column desktop layout** (M2, M12). This halves page length and fills the empty right side.
3. **Fix the compare silhouette scaling** so the drawing matches the computed sentence (B1, P9).
4. **Rebuild the home page:** a stronger hero (M5), Evolution Story with headline first in a 3×2 grid without notes (M3), and Explore Odisha as image cards (M4).
5. **Full-bleed alternating section bands** with an inner container (M6).
6. **Rework the timeline:** top-aligned, capped-height cards, tinted and shorter era art, edge fade and position indicator, no arrow buttons on mobile (M7, M8, M9).
7. **Compact, designed placeholders** instead of 560 px dashed boxes (M12).
8. **Type-scale discipline:** 14 px floor, a tokenised scale, no uppercase on the date pill (M10, M11).
9. **Redesign the "How big is it?" chart:** labels under figures, no duplicated caption, aligned note (M13).
10. **Remove dead ends and tidy the details:** "Soon" state in nav, non-link part chips, correct footer "Sources" link (M14); badge and citation kept on one line with sorted numbers (P1, P2).

## What I could not check

- **Live hover and animation:** screenshots are static. I judged hover and focus from the CSS, and did not watch the hero build animation or reduced-motion playback.
- **Menus and widgets:** I did not screenshot the open hamburger menu or open tooltips/panels. I checked them in the CSS/JS only.
- **Other widths and devices:** I did not test 768 px (the brief asked for 1440 and 375), real devices, or Safari/Firefox.
- **External links:** I did not open the GitHub issues link on About or the source URLs, which needs network access.
- **Lighthouse and performance budgets:** not run; this was out of scope for a visual audit.
