## Verdict

1. **One new site-wide blocker: the primary nav breaks at exactly 768 px on all 13 pages.** The hamburger
   is only shown below 767 px, but the horizontal menu needs ~881 px. At 768 the brand wraps to two lines
   and "About" is clipped at the viewport edge. The existing probe never caught this because it defaults
   to 1440 and 375 only.
2. **`map.html` genuinely drops its Leaflet controls onto the temple list** — the loader never waits for
   `leaflet.css`, so controls render unpositioned.
3. **Evidence-dot tooltips run up to 153 px off-screen** at 375 px. The width is capped; the position is not.
4. The typography, contrast, focus-ring, image-dimension and tap-target fundamentals **pass**. Body copy is
   18 px, prose is capped at 68ch, all 17 images carry width+height, and the focus ring clears 3:1.
5. **Most of the probe's 1,129 "target < 44px" hits and every "summary intersects…" hit are false positives.**
   See "Probe accuracy" — this matters more than any single finding, because it is hiding the real ones.

**Counts:** High 3 · Medium 6 · Low 6

## High-severity detail

**H1 — Primary nav overflows at 768 px (all 13 pages).**
`css/style.css:253` shows the hamburger only under `@media (max-width: 767px)`, so at 768 the full
horizontal menu renders. Measured at 768: logo 90 px (crushed from its natural 182 px, wrapping to two
lines), menu 651.4 px, nav padding 48 px → **789.5 px required inside a 753 px content box**;
`nav.scrollWidth` 765 > `clientWidth` 753, and the last item "About" ends at x=765.5, past the edge.
Un-crushed the row needs ~881 px.
Fix: `@media (max-width: 899px)` for both `.nav__toggle` and the `.nav__menu` drawer block.
900 px is already an established breakpoint in this codebase.

**H3 — Evidence-dot tooltip clips off-screen at 375 px.**
`.badge__tooltip` is `position:absolute; left:0` with `max-width: min(280px, calc(100vw - 32px))`. The
width cap works; the anchor does not. Measured on konark at a 360 px viewport: a badge at x=233 opens a
280 px tooltip spanning 233→513, i.e. **152.9 px beyond the right edge**. 31 badges on that page are
exposed to this.
Fix: below 768 px (or `@media (pointer: coarse)`), switch to `position: fixed; left: 16px; right: 16px;
width: auto; top: auto` so the tooltip is a full-width sheet; on desktop add a right-edge flip.

**H4 — Leaflet controls paint over the temple list (`map.html`).**
`js/map.js:98-117` appends the stylesheet `<link>` and the `<script>`, then resolves the promise on
**`script.onload` only** — the stylesheet is never awaited. `L.map()` therefore builds controls and panes
before `leaflet.css` applies, so `.leaflet-control-container` has no `position:absolute` and the controls
fall into normal flow on top of `.map-list`. Measured real (painted) overlaps: zoom-in 44×42 px over
`h3` "Jagannath Temple" and zoom-out over `p.map-list-meta` at 375; `li.map-list-item` over the footer
"Methodology" link at 1440. This is permanent, not just a flash, if the CDN is blocked.
Fix: resolve only after both load —
```js
const cssReady = new Promise(r => { link.onload = r; link.onerror = r; });
script.onload = () => cssReady.then(() => resolve(window.L));
```

## Medium-severity detail

**H5 — `.section` centring applied twice on compare and glossary.**
`css/style.css:136-137` sets `padding-left: max(var(--space-5), calc((100% - var(--content-max)) / 2 + var(--space-5)))`.
Percentages in padding resolve against the *containing block*, which is `main` (1425 px at 1440), so the
padding computes to 136.5 px — correct for a full-bleed section. But `css/pages/compare.css:3` and
`css/pages/glossary.css:3-6` additionally give that **same element** `max-width: var(--content-max); margin: 0 auto`,
adding a 112.5 px auto margin. The two stack: content lands at **249 px** instead of 137 px, visibly out
of line with the page's own H1. `timeline.css` avoids this by not putting `.section` on that element.
Fix: remove `max-width`/`margin` from `.compare` and `.glossary` (the `.section` padding already caps
content at `--content-max`).

**H2 — Home hero LCP element hidden for 3.6 s.**
`css/style.css:767` — `.hero-visual__image { opacity: 0; animation: hero-image-in 0.6s ease forwards; animation-delay: 3.6s; }`
while `index.html:205` marks the same image `loading="eager" fetchpriority="high"`. The browser is told to
fetch it at top priority and then told to hide it for 3.6 s, so the largest contentful paint cannot land
until ~4.2 s. Verified: at first paint the hero is blank apart from a few stroke-dash dots; at an 8 s
virtual-time budget it renders correctly. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is already handled properly.
Fix: cut the build-up to ~1.2 s total, or start the sequence from JS once the image has decoded so the
still image is never the thing being waited on.

**H6 — `404.html` absolute asset URLs.** See the 404 table. Note this also violates the relative-URL rule
in `CLAUDE.md` §5. `dev/preview/404.html` has the same two lines.

## Probe accuracy (act on this first)

`scripts/qa-probe.mjs` is sound but three gaps are currently masking real defects. **Do not "fix" the
site against its raw output.**

1. **Default widths are `1440,375`** (`qa-probe.mjs:13`), so the 768 px nav break — a blocker on every
   page — was invisible. Recommend defaulting to `375,768,1024,1440`.
2. **Categories C and E never check paintability.** They skip `display:none` and zero-size boxes, and they
   skip non-summary children of a *closed* `<details>` — but they do not handle a `<details>` nested inside
   another closed `<details>`. Chrome gives that content a real layout box with
   `content-visibility: hidden`, so it measures as visible but never paints. Proven on konark: 4
   "For curious grown-ups" summaries have non-zero rects, `checkVisibility()` false, and
   `outerDetails ["See 8 more"] open=false`. **Every `summary intersects …` finding on konark, mukteshwar
   and jagannath-puri is a false positive.** Fix: gate categories C and E on
   `el.checkVisibility({ checkVisibilityCSS: true, contentVisibilityAuto: true, opacityProperty: true, visibilityProperty: true })`.
3. **Category E counts inline prose links.** Of 1,129 hits, classification shows the overwhelming majority
   are inline `[n]` citation links inside sentences, which WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8 explicitly exempts (and the
   44 px rule is 2.5.5, AAA). Per page at 375: index 19 inline / 2 standalone, konark 58 / 4,
   compare 25 / 2, **glossary 0 / 0**. The only genuine standalone small targets are the `[n]` citation
   markers (~14×21 px) and Leaflet's attribution links (~16 px tall). Fix: report inline and standalone
   separately; the standalone `[n]` markers are still worth enlarging for 8–12-year-old fingers.

**Screenshot caveat for future sessions:** `--window-size=375,...` is unreliable on Windows — Chrome clamps
the window to ~500 px and then crops the capture, which fabricates "clipped text" that does not exist.
Confirmed: `map.html` looked badly broken at 375 that way, while a true 375 px iframe measured
`clientWidth` 360 with zero overflow. Use the `HANDOFF.md` iframe wrapper for anything under ~500 px.

## Top 3 prioritised polish recommendations

1. **Move the nav breakpoint to 900 px and re-run the probe at four widths.** One CSS media-query edit
   clears a clipped, brand-wrapping nav on all 13 pages at the single most common tablet width, and
   widening the probe default stops the next regression hiding in the same blind spot. Highest
   ratio of reach to effort on the site.
2. **Make the two interactive surfaces trustworthy: await `leaflet.css`, and turn the mobile tooltip into a
   fixed sheet.** These are the only places where a child actually taps something and gets a broken
   result — controls stacked on text, or an explanation 153 px off-screen. Both are small, local fixes
   (one promise, one media query) to `js/map.js` and `.badge__tooltip`.
3. **Unstack the double centring on compare/glossary and let the home hero arrive quickly.** The 249 px
   indent is the most visible "unfinished" cue on desktop, and a 3.6 s blank hero is the first thing every
   visitor sees. Copy the `timeline.css` container pattern, and cut the hero build-up to ~1.2 s.

*Runner-up, content not layout:* the era cards still leak "(Brown)" and "per Panigrahi" into the kid-facing
story text on home and compare — the one remaining story-voice violation visible above the fold.
That belongs in the fact-checker pipeline, not in markup.

## Corrections to the original pass

Two findings were investigated further while fixing and turned out to be wrong. Recording them so
nobody re-"fixes" them later.

**Withdrawn — H4 "Leaflet controls paint over the temple list".** The root cause given (leaflet.css
never awaited) was wrong, and so was the symptom. Re-measured with the page fully settled:
leaflet.css loads (124 rules), `.leaflet-top` is correctly `position: absolute`, and the zoom
buttons sit at y 1223–1267 *inside* `#mapContainer` (y 1210–1444). Nothing paints over the list.
The apparent overlap was a probe artifact: `.map-list-container` is capped at `max-height: 600px`
with `overflow-y: auto`, and `getBoundingClientRect()` reports the **full, unclipped** geometry of
list items that have been scrolled out of that container — so items reported coordinates on top of
the map that no reader can ever see. Fixed in the probe (see below), not in the page.
*The `js/map.js` change was kept*, but as hardening rather than a bug fix: resolving on
`script.onload` alone still allows a real race where leaflet.js executes before leaflet.css applies,
which would flash unpositioned controls. It now waits for both, and falls back to list-only if
either fails.

**Withdrawn — H6 "404.html absolute asset URLs".** This is deliberate and documented in
`scripts/build/lib/context.mjs`: GitHub Pages serves 404.html for unmatched paths at *any* depth, so
a relative `css/style.css` would resolve against the missing path and 404. Making it relative would
trade a local-preview nuisance for a production bug. The consequence is real but acceptable — in
local preview 404.html renders the *deployed* stylesheet, which is why it is the only page still
reporting `nav__soon` at 11.2px and sub-44px nav links. **Those 404-only numbers describe the live
site, not this working tree, and will clear once these fixes are pushed.**

**Probe accuracy, third gap (found while fixing).** Category C had no notion of clipping at all.
Both the nested-`<details>` case and the scrolled-list case are now handled by clipping every rect
against its scrolling/hidden ancestors before comparing (`visibleRect()`), which also handles
*partially* scrolled elements — only the on-screen part of an element can overlap anything.
