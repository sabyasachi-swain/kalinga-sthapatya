# Lingaraja: story rewrite (2026-09-25)

Tiers, sources, locators, ids, element order and media keys are unchanged. All 22 claim texts were
reviewed; 15 of them named a scholar in the reader-facing `text` and had the name moved to `note`
(a `note` key was added where none existed before). The patron attribution stays uncertain
(Yayati II + Uddyota Kesari, per Panigrahi); the natamandira 1099–1104 CE claim, already rejected by
the fact-checker, was not reintroduced. `facts.height` and `sections.size` stay `null`.
