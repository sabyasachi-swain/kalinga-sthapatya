# Parasuramesvara: story rewrite (2026-09-19)

Tiers, sources, locators, ids and media keys are unchanged. Nothing was merged or dropped: 16 claims were rewritten. Two claims moved within their section: in `special`, the planet slab now comes first, and in `influences` the order is primer, cousins, then far links. Scholar names now appear only in `note`.

| facts.date (001) | Scholars disagree… Panigrahi c. 650… Brown end of 8th c… Fergusson 500… Mitra 9th c. | No carved record tells us the year… clues in its style and the shapes of its carved letters. "Letter shapes" is the "palaeography of the inscriptions on its door", and "style" is Brown's "judging mainly from its appearance". The fact that no inscription is known was already in the approved note. |
