## B) Conflicts

1. **Markandesvara Temple: Puri vs. Bhubaneswar**
   - **View 1 (Puri)**: Centrally protected by the ASI as an ancient monument on the southern embankment of the sacred Markandeya tank in Puri town (*Antiquities of Orissa*, Vol. II, p. 118; ASI Monument List). Coordinates from OpenStreetMap: Node 6761287316 (`lat="19.8103127" lon="85.8150163"`).
   - **View 2 (Bhubaneswar)**: Often discussed in Kalinga architectural studies as a roughly contemporary 8th-century deul situated on the south-western bank of Bindusarovar tank in Bhubaneswar (*Hindu Temple Art of Orissa*, Vol. I, p. 38). It is not centrally protected by the ASI and is not indexed as an independent feature in OpenStreetMap.
   - **Resolution for interactive map**: The interactive map should clarify that two distinct historic temples bear this name in the Kalinga tradition; if focusing on Centrally Protected Monuments, the Puri Markandesvara temple is the ASI monument, whereas the Bhubaneswar Markandesvara temple is a state-endowment shrine on the Bindusagar.

2. **Administrative District Classification: Historical Puri District vs. Modern Khordha District**
   - **View 1 (Historical academic texts)**: Debala Mitra (1958) and Thomas E. Donaldson (1985) describe Bhubaneswar and Hirapur as belonging to Puri District: "Bhubaneswar... is situated in the Puri District of Orissa" (Mitra, p. 1); "Hirapur, a village about fifteen kilometres south-east of Bhubaneswar in Puri District" (Donaldson, Vol. I, p. 248).
   - **View 2 (Modern administrative reality)**: On 1 April 1993, the former undivided Puri District was bifurcated into three separate districts: Puri, Khordha (or Khurda), and Nayagarh. Bhubaneswar and Hirapur now fall under Khordha District, while Konark, Puri town, and Chaurasi remain in Puri District. Current ASI lists reflect this change by listing Bhubaneswar monuments under "Khurda" district.

3. **Protection Status of Kapilesvara Temple**
   - **View 1 (Pre-2023 status)**: For decades, Kapilesvara Temple remained outside the central protection list of the ASI, managed primarily by the Kapilesvara Temple Trust / State Endowment Department.
   - **View 2 (Post-2023 status)**: The Archaeological Survey of India and Ministry of Culture issued Gazette Notification S.O. 2119(E) on 5 May 2023, officially declaring Kapilesvara Temple a Monument of National Importance under the AMASR Act, 1958.
