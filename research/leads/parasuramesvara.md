B) Conflicts
- **Pabhaga Mouldings Nomenclature**: Mano Mohan Ganguly (Orissa and Her Remains) uses the terms *Pada*, *Kumuda*, and *Basanta* to describe the three base mouldings of the temple. In contrast, modern architectural descriptions (and ASI reports) use the terms *khura*, *kumbha*, and *pata*.
- **Tower Height**: Percy Brown (and general consensus) often gives the height as approximately 12.80 m (or 40.25 ft). Some technical conservation reports from the late 19th/early 20th century, such as those by M.H. Arnott, have cited 44 ft.
- **Ratha Plan**: There is a structural duality where the lower wall (*bada*) is *triratha* (having three projections), while the spire (*gandi*) above it is *pancharatha* (five projections), leading to early debates on its classification before scholars like Donaldson recognized it as a transitional characteristic.
