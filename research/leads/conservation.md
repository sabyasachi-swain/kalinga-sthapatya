## **B) Conflicts**

* **The construction, consecration, and collapse timeline of Konark's main tower:**  
  * *View 1 (Debated/Controversial):* Scholars acknowledge that fundamental questions regarding the temple's timeline, completion, and specific causes of structural failure remain unsettled.  
    * Quote: "Perhaps few temples in India have remained as controversial as Konark in vital matters such as construction, worship, and causes of its downfall. Did the builders finish the temple? Was it consecrated? When did the temple fall down? What are the outstanding features of Konark? A number of questions arise which form a popular subject of discussion among the scholars and common people."1.  
  * *View 2 (Specific Incident/Established):* Despite the broader uncertainties regarding the timeline of its initial decay, a specific date is assigned to the collapse of the *final* remaining fragment of the main tower.  
    * Quote: "The main temple, which was even higher than the jagamohana or vestibule, gradually disintegrated and its remaining fragment was brought down by a strong gale in October, 1848."1.
