B) Conflicts
None identified among the strictly accepted sources retrieved. Debala Mitra presents a clear picture without citing major disagreements for the extracted facts, except noting that "A number of reasons have been put forth for the possible cause of the collapse of the sikhara... general consensus, however, is that the structure crumbled gradually, the beginning of the decay initiated by the desecration of the temple by invaders."
